About English to Urdu Translator

Language Translator is a free online translation tool built to make communication between English and Urdu fast, clear, and accessible from any browser.

What it does

You can type or paste text, speak into your microphone, capture text with your camera, or upload an image or text file to get an instant translation. You can also listen to the translated result using the built-in audio button, copy it to your clipboard, or share it.

Who it is for

This translator is designed for students, travelers, professionals, and anyone who needs quick, reliable English to Urdu or Urdu to English translations on mobile phones, tablets, or desktop computers.

How it works

Simply choose your source and target languages, enter your content using text, voice, or an image, and click Translate / Analyze. Your request is processed securely and the translation is displayed immediately.
