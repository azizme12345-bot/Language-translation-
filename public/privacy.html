Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Language Translator processes information when you visit and use our website, how text, speech, and image data are handled, and how third-party services such as Google Gemini AI and Google AdSense operate.

What information is collected or processed

When you use the translator, any text you type or paste, audio recorded from your microphone, photos taken with your camera, or uploaded image and plain text (.txt) files are processed solely to fulfill your requested translation, speech transcription, image text extraction, or text-to-speech playback. Text files selected from your device are read locally in your browser before the text is sent for translation. We do not require user accounts, names, or passwords to use the translator.

How text and media are processed

Text, audio recordings, and images submitted for translation are sent securely to our backend translation endpoints (/api/translate, /api/transcribe, /api/analyze-image, and /api/tts), which use the Google Gemini API to generate translated text, recognize speech, extract text from images, and provide audio playback. We do not permanently store your translated text, audio, or uploaded images on our servers.

Browser storage

Your browser may store basic preferences locally—such as your selected light or dark theme and an optional custom API key if you choose to enter one—using localStorage so your preferred settings are remembered on your next visit.

Google AdSense and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements that help keep this tool free to use. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. You can learn more about how Google uses data for advertising at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads, and you may opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings or your browser settings.

Data security and user precautions

All translation requests are handled over standard web connections, and we limit processing to what is needed to fulfill each request. Because online translation relies on external AI processing, we recommend that you avoid submitting highly confidential personal, medical, or financial information.

Children's privacy and policy updates

This service is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements or legal requirements and post changes on this page with an updated revision date.
