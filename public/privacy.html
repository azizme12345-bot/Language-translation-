Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Language Translator handles your information when you use our website.

How text and media are processed

Text, audio recordings, and images submitted for translation are sent securely to our translation service to generate the translated output and are not permanently stored on our servers.

Browser storage

Your browser may store basic preferences locally—such as your selected light or dark theme—using localStorage so your preferred appearance is remembered on your next visit.

Google AdSense and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.
